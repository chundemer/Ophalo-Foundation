# Welcome to the OpHalo Keep Pilot

## Before our onboarding session

Thank you for being one of our first business partners. Keep is designed to give your team one
trusted place to capture customer needs, follow work through, and retain the important context
around each job.

This is an early pilot, not the full product vision. The core workflow is ready for real use, and
we will be direct about what is available today, what is arriving next, and where your feedback
will shape the product.

## What Keep can help with now

Keep helps your team make sure service requests are known, owned, acted on, and not forgotten.

- Capture a new request from a call, text, email, voicemail, walk-in, referral, or other customer
  contact.
- Keep customer, service-location, request, and work context together.
- Give the team a clear record of the request, follow-up, updates, completed work, and history.
- Help office staff see work that needs attention and reduce the chance that a customer waits for a
  response.
- Let field staff record factual work without exposing office pricing information.

### Your business request link

One of the most useful pilot features is your business request link. You can share it wherever
customers already find or contact you—on your website, social profiles, text messages, email
signature, or printed material.

When a customer uses the link, their request comes into Keep ready for your team to review instead
of requiring someone to retype information from another channel. It will not replace every way a
customer contacts you; calls and texts still matter. It gives you a simple way to capture more of
that work in one place from the start.

During onboarding, we will test the link together and decide where it makes the most sense for your
business to share it first.

## What is coming soon

We expect the financial-request export to arrive during your onboarding week. This will support a
clear handoff of reviewed financial work into your existing accounting process. We will confirm the
release with you when it is ready and show you the exact workflow before asking you to rely on it.

Other improvements are planned as the pilot teaches us where Keep can remove the most friction.
Those plans are not promises of a finished all-in-one system: Keep does not currently replace your
accounting system, invoice customers, take payments, manage inventory, or provide every dispatch
and scheduling function.

## What we will do in onboarding

Our session will focus on your real work, not a generic software tour. Together we will:

1. Map how a request reaches your business today and where information gets lost or retyped.
2. Set up or confirm your business details, team access, and request link.
3. Walk through one or two real examples from new request to recorded/completed work.
4. Identify what Keep can take off your team’s plate now and what should remain in your current
   process for the pilot.
5. Agree on a simple first-week workflow and a direct way to share feedback or report issues.

## Please bring or send before the meeting

If possible, send short answers to these questions before we meet:

1. How do new customer requests reach you today?
2. Where do requests most often get lost, delayed, or require someone to re-enter information?
3. What information do you wish you had before assigning, dispatching, or completing work?

It will also help to have:

- the names and email addresses of the initial team members who need access;
- one or two recent, representative customer requests we can use as examples;
- the places you may want to share your request link; and
- a description of the current accounting handoff you use after work is completed.

## How to work with us during the pilot

Please tell us when something feels unclear, slower than your existing process, or missing a fact
you need to do your job. The most useful feedback is specific: what you were trying to accomplish,
what happened instead, and what you expected.

We will treat your feedback as operating input, not as a burden placed on you. Our goal is to make
Keep fit the practical work of your team while staying transparent about its current boundaries.

We look forward to working through your first real workflows together.
